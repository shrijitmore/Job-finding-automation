Tidepool Finance is a DeFi lending protocol on Ethereum and Base.

You will write and audit Solidity smart contracts, build test suites in Foundry, and work with auditors before each launch.

Requirements: 2+ years of Solidity, Foundry or Hardhat, deep understanding of the EVM, DeFi protocol experience. Familiarity with TypeScript and ethers.js for tooling.

To apply, email jobs@tidepool.fi with your GitHub and a contract you are proud of.
